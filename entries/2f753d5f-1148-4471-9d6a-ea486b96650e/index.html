New Year New Hope

5/28/2026

Hello, Again.

Yes, this is again for the whatever-th time I started writing a blog-ish thing. And this time, I called it "Entries".



The fresh new year.

2026 is a brand new year to me, is the year I truly started to heal, to see myself honestly, and also, is the year I've started making myself grow toward aspects different than what I am used to.


I started a new project, Open Bodycam, the largest personal project I've worked on (yet), with whole eco-system level engineering on the horizon, this is a way back to what's similar to my origin, but also expands my vision, skills and ability, it is a good challenge for me to push myself again.


And yes, I started grinding LeetCode in 2026 (more seriously), funny, right? I've been messing around with software engineering for over 10 years, yet I find myself stuck on some easy-medium DSA coding problems. But somehow I discovered the interesting side of DSA grinding, having be honest, I still developed no passion in it, just find how I see and treat it differently than before.



Career

As I've quitted my job last year, I've been thinking what my past 7 years, I have positioned myself as web developer since my first job, and did developed deep passion and skills in it, but this thought is also what limited me. What philosophy draw me to become a software engineer is to create solutions for everyone's daily life, so that's where I am heading, not just do the web, but to scale myself toward larger scaled engineering aspects.


It is challenging though, but that's what growth takes, and I am happy that I found that it's time to move forward, further.
