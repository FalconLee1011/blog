Toward A Different Tomorrow

7/16/2026

Becoming the one in the shadow

I've started my "shadow workforce life" this week, after long long career seeking, I have to admit that this is both expected and unexpected at the same time.

The high bar

Why it is both expected and unexpected ? Such a "Schrödinger" sound, well, that's because I've set this time's hunting bar a bit high, not for anything but seeking challenges and growth.

Throwing myself out of the comfort zone is risky, but after half year of rest and resetting myself, I find that's what I want.

Throughout my career, I've created a lot of things from 0 to 1, drafted convention of development, building libraries, pipelines and tests to ensure productivity and quality, but one common thing is that majority of them are around WEB Tech.

The "Don't get me wrong" part

I did not in fact at all lost interest or passion in web development (especially in human interface and experience), but I think it is the time to take a step toward something different at work.

I (will) still deliver web-related stuff as personal projects, but shifting the direction of career is what I am seeking, because it is a way to push one's growth, a efficient one for me.

Lessons took

During this time's job hunt, I've took a lot of lessons, probably the most growth I ever had yet, there will be another Entry for this topic, where I developed some knowledge that I find myself lack during the preparations, interviews, and tests.

The thing is, I was at somewhere wondering where to go, and these lessons taught me the answers. Although there are a lot of seem-to-be-awesome chances did not work, these valuable insights is something I gain, will dig more and I would never forget.



Toward a different tomorrow

For the first half of 2026, I would say it was for moving toward a different tomorrow, and for the second half, I hope it is for a better tomorrow.

I took a lot of time to rethink and rest myself for the past year, to create a more healthy, solid, and adventurous/challenging tomorrow. And I think the effect is quite impressive (yes, I still have an arrogant side)

I believe I've gained various different angle to view people and things that I never thought I would, these angles will never be enough, I will treat them more valuable than ever.

Last but not least, I hope these insights would help me to gain a different, a better tomorrow.
